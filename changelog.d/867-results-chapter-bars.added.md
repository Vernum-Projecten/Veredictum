- The console results page draws the outcome as chapter bars above the rows:
  one shaded header per chapter and one stacked bar per capability folder,
  with passed, failed, advisory, errored and skipped counts beside each bar.
  The chart is server-rendered SVG in the Okabe-Ito palette, with a glyph
  for every outcome, and `?view=options` draws RECOMMENDED, OPTIONS and the
  spec boundary instead of BASE, each tier read by chapter and folder with
  its options and TRIAL rows indented under their folder. Each bar links to
  its rows, and the rows are grouped the same way, the rows resting on text
  reported upstream as defective closing the list. Each row shows its
  formalism, TRIAL and report marks, and an advisory row has its own stat
  card and is never counted as failed.
