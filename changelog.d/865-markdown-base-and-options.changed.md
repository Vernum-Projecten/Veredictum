- The Markdown summary `export` writes is laid out for a person reading it on
  GitHub. A summary table counts the rows of BASE, RECOMMENDED, OPTIONS and
  the spec boundary. BASE follows by chapter, band and section, each level a
  table of counts, and the options follow the same chapters and bands as
  tables naming the arm the statement selected. Passed rows are counted, not
  listed. Each failed, advisory or errored row is a collapsed block that opens
  on its explanation as a table, with the cited sentence quoted. Skipped rows
  are counted by reason. The cases resting on TRIAL text sit in their band
  under OPTIONS.
