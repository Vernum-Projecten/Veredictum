- A performance workload is a versioned document under `perf/workloads/`, published as `schemas/perf-workload.schema.json`. Loading it refuses, naming the file and the field, shares that do not sum to 100 %, a session over 10 s of mean think time, an operation or query outside the vocabulary, a payload missing from `perf/payloads.yaml`, a query that does not parse or binds its parameters wrongly, a `requires` token that names no capability folder or option arm, a mix outside the 10:1 to 50:1 read band, a qualification limit looser than the ruled default, and a planned run longer than 10 minutes after seeding.
- `veredictum perf-workloads` prints each workload's expanded per-operation mix, read:write ratio and planned run range; `--out` writes the byte-deterministic manifest of the pinned workloads with their sha256 digests (`schemas/perf-workloads.schema.json`) and the page generated from it, and `--workload` checks one document of your own, labelled `custom` unless it matches a pinned digest.
