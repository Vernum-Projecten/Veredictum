## clinical-mix v1 (Provisional)

A hospital front end at its busy hour: clinicians opening charts (a partly-open session of subject lookup, summary query, document reads and an observation trend), ward boards and problem lists polling AQL, the monitoring, order, result and correction writes of ward work, and the definition, folder, tag, demographic and bootstrap traffic around them. Carried over from the 24 journeys of perf/journeys.yaml without their minute-scale offsets. Provisional, for SEC ratification.
